About this property

Private Pool, Stunning Mountain Views & Character-Building Amenities

Minutes from the festival. Years from the photos.

Welcome to your PGA West escape, where panoramic mountain views meet highly negotiable standards. Enjoy an open-plan kitchen, a private casita, and the rare opportunity to reconnect with nature without leaving the pool.

The primary suite features our signature floor-level sleep experience and bespoke contractor-bag blackout blinds. In the games room, a lovingly distressed pool table delivers a perfectly predictable result: all the balls go to the same corner.

Spend your evenings poolside, discussing who was responsible for the booking. Our private patio offers a carefully curated selection of chairs from several different decades.
